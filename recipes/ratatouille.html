Ratatouille


Home

Description

Ratatouille is the art of spending 45 minutes meticulously layering squash just to prove a cartoon mouse isn't a better chef than you. It is a vibrant, elegant French spiral that tastes delicious, looks beautiful, and aggressively forces you to eat your vegetables

Ingredients

For the Tomato Sauce Base

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 bell pepper (any color), finely chopped
  • 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp fresh basil, chopped
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

For the Vegetable Rounds

  • 2 zucchini, sliced into thin rounds
  • 2 yellow squashes, sliced into thin rounds
  • 2 Chinese eggplants, sliced into thin rounds
  • 4 plum tomatoes, sliced into thin rounds

For the Herb Drizzle

  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Set your oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Sauté the chopped onion, garlic, and bell pepper until soft (about 5 minutes). Stir in the crushed tomatoes, season with salt and pepper, and let it simmer for 5 minutes. Stir in the fresh basil, then spread this sauce evenly across the bottom of a round baking dish.
  3. Alternately arrange slices of zucchini, yellow squash, eggplant, and plum tomatoes in a tightly packed, overlapping spiral pattern over the sauce, working from the outside of the dish toward the center.
  4. Whisk together the herb drizzle ingredients (olive oil, thyme, salt, and pepper) and brush it evenly over the top of the vegetable slices. Cover the dish loosely with foil.
  5. Bake covered for 40 minutes, then remove the foil and bake for an additional 20 minutes until the vegetables are tender and the edges are slightly golden.
  6. Let it cool slightly before serving as a side dish or main over crusty bread.
