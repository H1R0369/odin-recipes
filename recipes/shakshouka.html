Description

A visually stunning single-skillet presentation showcasing perfectly poached eggs nestled inside a rich, spiced pepper and tomato sauce

Ingredients

  • 4-5 large Egg
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 medium Onion, diced
  • 1 large Red bell pepper, chopped
  • 3 cloves Garlic, minced
  • 1 can (14 oz) Crushed or diced tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • ¼ tsp Chili powder or Harissa paste (optional for heat)
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper (to taste)
  • For Garnish: Fresh cilantro, parsley, or crumbled feta cheese

Steps

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet or cast-iron pan over medium heat. Add the diced onion and chopped bell pepper. Cook for about 5 minutes until soft and translucent.
  2. Stir in the minced garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, and chili powder. Sauté for another 1 to 2 minutes until the spices become highly fragrant.
  3. Pour in the crushed tomatoes and tomato paste. Stir well to combine the ingredients. Reduce the heat to low-medium and let the sauce simmer for about 10 minutes until it thickens slightly. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Use a large spoon to make 4 to 5 small wells or pockets directly in the simmering tomato sauce. Gently crack one egg into each well
  5. Turn the heat down to low, cover the pan with a lid, and let it cook for 5 to 8 minutes. You want the egg whites to be completely set while the yolks remain beautifully runny.
  6. Remove from heat. Top the dish with fresh chopped cilantro, parsley, or crumbled feta cheese. Serve hot directly from the skillet alongside crusty warm bread.
